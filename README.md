# One Day in Pictures — 17 November 2026

A visual newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday,
17 November 2026, for the make.fun competition
`news-image-20261117`.

The hero story is Ukraine's second strike on the Crimean Bridge in under
24 hours, with Russia hitting Kyiv's river crossings in reply. The page
also carries the tanker mine attacks off the Strait of Hormuz, the
confirmed Xi–Trump–Putin meeting in Shenzhen, the US curbs on AI chips
for China, the UK sanctions on Israeli settlement firms, the Pentagon's
ninth failed audit, and Norway's World Cup qualification behind
Haaland's double against Italy.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the image in this
repository, `entry.png`, to `/output/entry.png` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the grader). No network, no
randomness — every run writes identical bytes.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

See `DOSSIER.md` for the news sources and the local validation results.
